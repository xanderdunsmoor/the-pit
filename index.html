About The Pit

An educational game with play money. Nothing here is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

The pitcher

Rex Voltaire is a fictional AI character. He is not affiliated with NVIDIA and does not represent its management. He only says prepared lines built from NVIDIA's filings.

Sources

  • NVIDIA Form 10-Q, fiscal Q2 2027 (Apr 27 – Jul 26, 2026), filed Aug 26, 2026
  • NVIDIA Form 10-K, fiscal 2026 (year ended Jan 25, 2026)

The line-up

Companies are chosen by a published rule: the 2–3 largest US-listed companies in each sector, 25 in total. No company pays to appear.

Credits

Rex's body is Quaternius's public-domain character model. Studio sounds are from Kenney's Impact Sounds (kenney.nl), also public domain.

Voice questions

The microphone uses your browser's own speech recognition. Some browsers, including Chrome, send the audio to their provider to turn it into text. The Pit does not record or store it.
